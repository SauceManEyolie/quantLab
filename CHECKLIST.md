# The 60-Second Pre-Flight

Keep this open. Run it before **every** trade — in QuantLab, on FOMO, anywhere.
It takes a minute. It is the whole difference.

---

## 1. The four blanks — fill all four or don't trade

If you can't answer every one in writing, you don't have a trade. You have a hope
with money attached.

- [ ] **WHAT** am I buying?
- [ ] **HOW MUCH**, in dollars — decided from my risk rule, not my excitement?
- [ ] **WHERE AM I WRONG** — the exact price that disproves this?
- [ ] **WHERE AM I DONE** — the exact price or rule that ends it?

> Blank #3 is the one that matters. If you cannot name a price at which you'd
> admit the idea failed, then there is no outcome that will ever make you sell,
> which means you will hold it to zero.

---

## 2. The five disqualifiers — any one of these, walk away

- [ ] **Already up more than 100% in 24h.** You'd be buying from whoever is about to sell.
- [ ] **24h volume more than 10× liquidity.** Consistent with wash trading. Check both numbers.
- [ ] **Liquidity smaller than the largest holder's position.** They cannot exit without
      collapsing it, so their gain is notional and *you* are the exit they need.
- [ ] **I found it because it was moving.** Movement is not a reason. It's an advertisement.
- [ ] **I feel urgency.** Real setups are still there in an hour. Urgency is manufactured.

---

## 3. The sizing rule — no exceptions

```
dollars at risk = 2% of the account
position size   = dollars at risk ÷ (stop distance as a fraction)
```

- Never more than **25%** of the account in one position.
- **A position with no stop risks 100% of itself.** For a memecoin, assume the
  whole position is the risk — because it is.
- More volatile → wider stop → **smaller** position. Never the reverse.

---

## 4. Where the decision gets made

**Before** you open the position. Not during.

Once you're holding, you are no longer a neutral observer — you're someone with a
position who wants to be right. Every rule you set after that point is negotiable,
and you will negotiate.

Write the exit down first. Then honour it without reopening the argument.

---

## 5. When you feel the pull

You will. That's not a flaw, it's how the products are designed.

1. **Say what you're feeling out loud.** "I feel like I'm missing out." Naming it
   drops most of its force.
2. **Set a 60-minute timer** and do something else. Almost every trade that survives
   an hour of waiting was a real setup. Almost none of the others were.
3. **Ask: would I take this at 10× the size?** If no, you don't believe it — you're
   just scratching an itch. Take zero instead.
4. **Route it through Guided Trade.** If the app can't produce four numbers for it,
   that's your answer.

---

## The one sentence

**Movement is not a reason. Attention is not information. Urgency is manufactured.**

---

*Losing money is survivable and normal. Losing money without a rule you broke means
you learned nothing, which is the only genuinely expensive outcome.*
